## Featured Projects

### [BE WATER MY FRIEND](https://github.com/tttttracycui/BWMF--)

An AI-assisted investment research and decision-governance framework. It connects research, evidence, counter-evidence, risk checks, plan readiness, and post-decision review in one traceable workflow—while keeping humans accountable and trade execution out of scope.

`Next.js 16` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS 4` · `AI provider adapters` · `RBAC` · `Audit trails`

### [US LENS](https://github.com/tttttracycui/us-lens-daily)

An evidence-aware US market brief system that turns Obsidian research and AI-assisted analysis into a searchable, reader-friendly web archive. It includes a Markdown-to-JSON content pipeline, pre-market and post-market workflows, evidence filtering, responsive reading, and automated deployment.

`Next.js 16` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Node.js` · `Cloudflare Workers` · `Obsidian`
